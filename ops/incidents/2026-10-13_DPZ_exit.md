# Incident 2026-10-13 · DPZ PX-F (paper short iron butterfly): the primary exit did not fill

## Part 1 — expectations, written BEFORE reading what happened on Oct 14
Written 2026-10-14 10:5x ET from the local repo as of `d606079`, which holds only the Oct 13 state. **No pull
was made before this file was committed;** the commit timestamp proves it. Every number below comes from the
ledger's recorded Oct 13 quotes, or is a stated guess.

- **Entry, Oct 9.** Sold the 310 call and the 310 put, and bought the 360 call and the 260 put (exp. Oct 16),

- **Spot by put-call parity** at the 14:46 quotes: ≈ **298.8**, down about 2.8% from the 307.4 gate spot.

### My expectations for what happened on Oct 14 (written blind)
1. **The close retried** at the first paper-options run after 09:30 ET, at that run's natural.
   - P(filled by the Oct 14 close) = **0.65**.
   - Why not higher: a limit at the natural did not fill in 15+ minutes on Oct 13. So the paper engine does
     not reliably fill a 4-leg order at the sum of the far sides, or a quote went stale (the 360 call's last
     quote was 9 minutes old).
2. **If it filled on Oct 14:**
   - P&L per unit: central **+1.0** (+$100); 80% range −2.0 to +4.0.
   - The reasoning: one more day of decay helps the short fly, but at a natural with spreads this wide most
     of it is given back.
3. **If it is still open at expiry (Fri Oct 16):** D011 settles at intrinsic on the expiry close: P&L per unit
   = 17.60 − |S − 310| while 260 ≤ S ≤ 360. At S = 299 that is **+6.6**. This is better than any natural
   close, which itself shows that holding to expiry avoids the exit spread (D010 v2 found hold-to-expiry
   did not help on average).
4. **Classification:** whatever the fill time, the frozen report counts DPZ as an on-time exit. That is a
   blind corner (Part 3).

**Why I was wrong:** I priced the delay as a day of time decay at a roughly constant spot. A missed exit
is not a cost-of-waiting problem. **It turns an event trade into an unhedged directional position after
the event**: a short 310 put, with DPZ continuing down on day 2. The miss cost about **8 per unit, about
2.6% of spot**: from +0.32 at the on-time natural (+3.29 at the on-time mid) to about −7.7. That is several
times the whole short-straddle edge D010 measured.

## Part 3 — causes, blind corners, fixes

### Causes, in order of weight
1. **Scheduling.** GitHub's `*/10` cron dropped most of its fires: runs came at 14:46, 15:46 and then
   after 16:00 ET. The frozen code resends only on a LATER run after a 15-minute cancel, so with no later
   run inside the window there was no second attempt.
2. **A fill that never came, even at the natural.** Two closes at the sum of the far sides did not fill in
   15+ minutes. The common factor: **both wings had a zero bid**, so the close asks the paper engine to
   *sell* two legs at 0.00. The opening order, which bought the wings at their asks, filled within 32
   minutes.
   - **Hypothesis, not yet verified:** Alpaca's paper engine does not fill a multi-leg order with a
     zero-bid sell leg.
   - **If true, nearly every PX-F close hits this.** After the print the wings (about two implied moves out) are far out of
     the money with no bid, so every exit fails and the track silently becomes hold-to-expiry.
3. **The alarm came too late.** Health ran at 13:30 and 21:15 UTC, outside the 14:30–16:00 ET exit window.
4. **The accounting is blind.** `exit_late` is set by when the first close was SENT, not when it filled, so
   the frozen report counts DPZ as on time.

| # | fix | rule impact |

Five start times, because any one cron fire may drop | none: cadence only. The frozen 15-minute cancel and resend rules decide |
| 2 | `health.py`: **EXIT NOT FILLED** is RED from 15:15 to 15:55 ET on the reaction day, while it can still be saved; **EXIT MISSED** is AMBER after that. Health runs at 15:20 ET (EDT and EST) | none: ops |
| 3 | `registry/D011_exit_audit_v1.md` + `pipeline/paper_exit_audit.py`: every trade's on-time counterfactual (natural and mid, from the recorded quotes) next to the actual, and the delay cost | descriptive, frozen |

The close becomes the 2-leg buy-back of the short straddle | a NEW version, its own clock (rule 5). It waits on evidence about the paper engine (background research, 2026-10-14) |
