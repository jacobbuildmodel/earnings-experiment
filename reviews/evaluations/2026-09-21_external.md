# External evaluations — 2026-09-21 (design stage; no outcome existed)

Two independent evaluators each started cold, with only `reviews/external_evaluator_brief.md` and the
repository. Both were read-only, and both were fenced from `archive/` and from anything outside the
folder.
- **E1:** a Sonnet reviewer. It read the design and the code, and ran nothing.
- **E2:** an Opus reviewer with an empirical-finance lens. It ran `check.py`, timing-check cases, and
  size and power simulations.

Before them, two internal reviews by a second model (Sonnet) covered the pipeline (red team) and D005
plus the grader. Their fixes are in commits `c781a26`, `c5f8c6a` and `e61e01c`.

## Verdicts, in brief

- **E1:** would provisionally trust a positive result. Biggest threat: T2's blindness was checked on
  what it asked for, not on what it read, and the check worked to the day, not the minute. Same-day,
  post-snapshot web content could get through.
- **E2:** no CRITICAL findings. The blindness machinery is "unusually strong". Would not trust a
  positive Season A result, and would trust Season B only after fixes. Biggest threat: **low power
  (15–40% against realistic edges) combined with many printed verdicts.** A significant result would
  quite likely be false, and would overstate a true edge.

## Still open after this review

- Power is the binding constraint. One season cannot settle the question. This is registered (D005 v1
  §7, v2 §5), and the answer is pooling seasons under a future D005 version, never loosening the test.
- The EPS consensus basis across vendors is not pinned. No EPS-surprise factor may enter a rule until
  it is.
