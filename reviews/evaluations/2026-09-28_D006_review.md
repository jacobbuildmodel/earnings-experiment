**1. CRITICAL — univariate screen threshold (t ≥ 2.0) is below CLAUDE.md's mandated exploration bar,
and no FDR correction is applied at this stage.** Rule 7: "Exploration: false-discovery control and
t > 3." Rule v0's construction is exploration, yet R-SEL's screen uses t ≥ 2.0 with no BH step. With 10
correlated factors (H1/H2/H3 momentum-family, H6/H7 seasonality-family), at least one false positive is
likely. *Fix:* t > 3 with BH q < 0.10, or document an exemption.

**2. CRITICAL — H6/H8/H10 don't specify protection against restated XBRL comparatives.** 10-Qs carry
restated prior-year comparatives. If the pull takes whatever the current XBRL frame returns, past values
may reflect later filings. *Fix:* pin each value to filings available before the snapshot and state it
in §4.

**3. MAJOR — no pre-freeze projected N for TRAIN/VALIDATION after exclusions.** The per-event $2B floor
will exclude early events of recently grown names. *Fix:* run the build once and report counts only.

**4. MAJOR — the composite BUY share is never checked.** A mean of 10 ranks concentrates near 0.5;
correlated or lopsided factors, or missing-value defaults, can move the BUY share far from 50%.
*Fix:* report the BUY share on TRAIN and VALIDATION with a tripwire.

**5. MAJOR — step 7's rule choice is a max-of-two selection on VALIDATION.** The reported number is
optimistically biased. *Fix:* state that it is a selected-winner estimate.

**6. MAJOR — LR21 and LR22 dropped silently.** *Fix:* add them, or give a reason.

**7. MAJOR — H4 doesn't test LR08.** Realised volatility is a different construct (low-volatility
anomaly). *Fix:* re-label and warn.

**8. MODERATE — H10 overstates its LR27 citation.** LR27 is a composite. *Fix:* build the composite, or
re-cite H10.

**9. MAJOR — rule v0's frozen reference may not match the live universe if I1 is adopted.** *Fix:*
state that a universe change needs a new rule version.

**10. MODERATE — history-side two-source agreement is likely easier than live.** *Fix:* disclose; don't
use history exclusion rates as a proxy for live ones.

**11. MINOR/MODERATE — the missing-value default is a business-type confound.** H6 needs about 8
quarters of EPS, which young companies lack. *Fix:* report missingness by cluster/stage; consider
excluding events with many missing factors.

**12. MINOR — the preview universe may not equal the frozen D003 screen.** *Fix:* reconcile.

**13. MINOR — H8 tests a different claim than LR20.** *Fix:* don't claim it confirms or refutes LR20.
