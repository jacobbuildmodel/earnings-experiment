The result was produced by the cloud routine `earnings-d010-backtest` on branch `claude/d010-backtest`. This
is the review that STATE required before merging.

## Verification

- **Spec match:** I re-derived 200 randomly sampled valid events (seed 1) from `raw/`, checking that:
  - `short_exec` = (call bid_P + put bid_P − call ask_Q − put ask_Q) / S_P
  - the strike is the call delta closest to 0.50, ties going to the lower strike
  - the expiry is the first listed after R in P's chain

  **0 mismatches** on any of the three.
- **Determinism:** `run_d010.py` was re-run on the laptop. `results.json` is identical; only the line
  endings differ on Windows.
- **Voids:** they are counted by reason and year in the report. 5,853 of 7,107 events are `no_quote`. That
  is a data-coverage limit of DoltHub, not an economic result.

## What the report gets right, and what it misses

- **Right:** on all valid events, neither side of the straddle pays after bid/ask costs, and nothing meets
  the finding rule.
- **Missing, and it changes the reading: the costs are driven by illiquid names.**
  - The median relative spread of the traded legs is **21% of the option price at P and 31% at Q**, with
    a mean of 33% and 52%.
  - For comparison, our live gates show a median of 3.8% (12 pilot legs), and DPZ's live Alpaca quotes
    were 11% and 14%. DPZ was itself INELIGIBLE `wide_spread`.
  - D010 traded every event, including names the S2 gate would refuse.

## Reading

- **Long straddles lose robustly**, even on liquid names, which agrees with de Silva, Smith & So (2025). The
  +3.34% buyer result of Gao, Xing & Zhang (2018) does not survive real spreads on this sample.
- **The short side's edge is about the size of the spread.** It exists at mid on liquid names, and it
  vanishes or reverses when the spread is paid twice. So execution and liquidity decide it, not direction.
- Two things favour the live SH-X design over D010's exit:
  - **holding to expiry** skips the second, wider spread at Q
  - **D011's real fills** measure what is actually paid
