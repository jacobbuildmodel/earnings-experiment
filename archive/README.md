# ⛔ ARCHIVE — PRE v1, CLOSED 2026-09-13

**Do not read this folder while forming hypotheses, choosing factors, or making BUY / NO_BUY decisions.**

This is the complete record of the retired pre-earnings forecaster, moved here from the DRIVE repo on
2026-09-13 and committed byte-exact — 98 files, each verified by SHA-256 against its original.

## Why it is fenced

PRE v1 closed with no demonstrated edge — 14 of 27 blind forecasts correct, 51.9%, p = 1.00 against a
50% null. But the decisive reason was that **its scoring was changed four times while it was being
measured**, each change prompted by a loss, so its record belongs to no single model.

That means **any opinion it formed about which factors work is not evidence.** Reading it before forming
new hypotheses would carry that contamination straight into a clean experiment.

## What may be taken from it

**Facts about data sources and engineering controls** — already copied into this project's `CLAUDE.md`
(e.g. yfinance `.calendar` is a day late for after-close reporters; SEC requires a User-Agent header).
**Nothing about which factors predict returns.**
