## What was wrong

- The first run (Mon 11-02 07:28 ET) read the shadow grade committed Fri 10-30 21:52 ET (`c2dc357`). That was the newest
  grade on `main` at the time: the next, `4d9affc`, landed Mon 11-02 18:16 ET, after the run.
- A shadow session counts as settled only from 06:00 ET the next day (`pipeline/shadow.py`, `settled()`), and the grader
  fires on weekday evenings (`.github/workflows/shadow.yml`, cron `40 22 * * 1-5`). So no grader ran between Friday's
  exits settling (Sat 06:00 ET) and the Stage 1 run.
- At `c2dc357` the events whose exit-A open was Fri 10-30 were ungraded and not VOID: SH-W 20, SH-M 114. The gated
  grade of Fri 20:57 ET (`e036eea`) left 5 gated events in the cut ungraded (the next grade, `c4c1e9f`, came Mon 22:00 ET).
- `stage1.py` dropped the ungraded shadow events without saying so. It reported "pending 6" for the gated read, but that
  6 also wrongly included CIFR, whose exit-A open is Mon 11-02, outside the cut.

## The fix (both committed before the re-run)

- `4383102`: `stage1.py` counts the events in the cut that are neither graded nor VOID, per read, prints them, and
  `--write` refuses while any remain. Test: `tests/test_stage1.py::test_pending_in_cut_is_counted_and_blocks_write`.
- `a6f7cc6`: an ungraded gated event that has no exit session yet is in the cut only if it entered before the cut day
  (exit A is at least one session after entry). Test: `test_cut_by_exit_a`.
